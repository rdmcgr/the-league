Wins over Points

Pass the belt
6 champions in 6 seasons

From 2020 through 2025, every season produced a different champion. With Nikki becoming a first-time champion in '25. About Damn Time indeed.

Last year, the league celebrated its 20th season — an achievement that, frankly, puts America’s 250th birthday this year in perspective. The United States may have a Constitution, national parks, and a moon landing (maybe), but it didn't survive two decades of veto arguments, thousands of roster moves (And that's just the tally from Rafi Bombs. He's a tinkerer.), and Team Winner’s heroic commitment to almost winning — a legacy of noble defeat not seen since the Confederacy tried to run a league without waiver rules.
